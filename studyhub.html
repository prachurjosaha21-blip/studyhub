Fractions are parts of a whole.

A fraction has a numerator on top and a denominator below. The denominator tells you how many equal parts make the whole; the numerator tells you how many parts you have. For example, 3/4 means three of four equal parts.

Try it: Draw a circle, split it into four equal parts, and shade three parts to show 3/4.
